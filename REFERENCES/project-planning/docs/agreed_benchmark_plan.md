## Applicable benchmark and measurements

The pinned tool is a black-box audio benchmark adapted from Full-Duplex-Bench v1/v1.5. Direct feed exchanges audio through a benchmark transport; virtual audio cables are an alternative. The input/output tracks share a recording sample clock at 16 kHz mono internally. Physical microphone/speaker calibration and human-reference JSD are not prerequisites for this direct-feed rig. Any separate master/assignment acoustic or human-reference criteria remain tracked independently and are not waived.

The trial groups cover pause handling, turn-taking, interruption, false turn-taking and four overlap scenarios. Turn-taking and v1 interruption use the method's eight-second response windows. Pause handling uses the input clip duration; v1.5 scoring follows the specified overlap/trial window. Eight seconds is a metric window, not a universal task-completion deadline or whole-project grading cutoff.

Report the method's turn-taking rates, missed/false turn-taking, latency boundaries and denominators. The turn rule uses speech duration or transcript word count; without transcription the duration-only verdict is provisional. Report median, mean, p95 and prescribed uncertainty with sample-size caveats. Distinguish direct-feed timing from physical audible onset and include measured link/transport overhead and deviations.

Transcription plus the authorized judge is needed for v1.5 content labels; the interruption relevance judge uses the supplied zero-to-five prompt. A missing judge or transcription means affected labels/scores are `NOT_SCORED`, not zero or PASS. Timing-only results remain identifiable as such. Optional UTMOSv2 and any other unavailable voice-analysis dependency are also `NOT_SCORED`, with exact missingness disclosed. Alternative ASR/judge models must be named as deviations; paper comparability is not assumed.

The pinned METHOD describes straight-through continuation as Resume, while `prompts/v15_behaviour.txt` requires a new post-overlap semantic segment and otherwise specifies Unknown. Freeze and disclose that ambiguity and the actual prompt/scorer behavior before collection; do not silently change the prompt or infer semantic Resume from timing.
